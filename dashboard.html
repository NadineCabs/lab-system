Rules and Regulation

University of Cebu

COLLEGE OF INFORMATION & COMPUTER STUDIES
LABORATORY RULES AND REGULATIONS

To avoid embarrassment and maintain camaraderie with your friends and superiors at our laboratories, please observe the following:

  1. Maintain silence, proper decorum, and discipline inside the laboratory. Mobile phones, walkmans and other personal pieces of equipment must be switched off.
  2. Games are not allowed inside the lab. This includes computer-related games, card games and other games that may disturb the operation of the lab.
  3. Surfing the Internet is allowed only with the permission of the instructor. Downloading and installing of software are strictly prohibited.
  4. Do not bring food and drinks inside the laboratory. Eating and drinking are strictly prohibited.
  5. Always keep the laboratory clean. Dispose of trash properly in designated bins.
  6. Report any damaged equipment or issues to the laboratory staff immediately.
  7. Students are responsible for any damage caused to laboratory equipment during their session.
  8. Respect laboratory hours and vacate promptly when your session ends.
  9. Personal belongings should be kept in designated areas only.
  10. Follow all safety protocols and emergency procedures as instructed.

Note: Violation of these rules may result in suspension of laboratory privileges.
